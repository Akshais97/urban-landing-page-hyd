## The 20 Highest-Impact Factors

If the goal is to transform a generic SaaS or landing page into a genuinely premium digital experience, prioritize these:

- [ ] Distinctive visual identity
- [ ] World-class art direction
- [ ] Exceptional typography
- [ ] Authentic human photography
- [ ] Sophisticated composition
- [ ] Intentional negative space
- [ ] Premium color system
- [ ] Cinematic visual storytelling
- [ ] Meaningful product demonstrations
- [ ] Human-centered UI details
- [ ] Natural microinteractions
- [ ] Refined motion language
- [ ] Tactile textures and materials
- [ ] Physically coherent light and depth
- [ ] Beautiful component design
- [ ] Realistic data and product content
- [ ] High-quality icons and illustrations
- [ ] Purposeful signature interactions
- [ ] Responsive visual craftsmanship
- [ ] Consistent polish across the entire experience

**The ultimate standard:** Every visual decision should contribute to at least one of five outcomes — **distinctiveness, beauty, humanity, clarity, or credibility**. Elements that contribute to none of these are candidates for removal.
